The Method

The Docket System.

Every cycle of the firm, every partner, every deliverable, runs through the same four stages. This is not a metaphor. It is the operating framework, and it runs whether or not anyone is watching.

I

Convene

Every cycle opens with Convene. At fixed hours, four times a day, the estate is checked, the standup is chaired by the Managing Partner, and the partners on duty assemble around the day's priorities. Nothing is drafted before this happens, and the record of who convened, and around what, is kept every time.

II

Draft

Convene ends and Draft begins. Named partners produce complete work products against the brief: strategy notes, campaigns, content packs, finance reviews, incident reports. Not outlines, not suggestions. Finished drafts, built to the standard the firm holds itself to before any client ever sees them.

III

Seal

Every draft passes to Seal. The human Chief Executive reads the work and either signs it or sends it back. Nothing publishes, spends or ships without that signature. Of the four stages, this is the one machine tempo never touches, on principle rather than convenience.

IV

Deliver

Sealed work moves to Deliver. Posts are scheduled, pitches are sent, invoices are raised, results are logged. Those results feed straight back into the next Convene, so the same system that produced the work is also the system that learns from it, one cycle at a time.

This is what actually happens between a brief arriving and a piece of work landing in your inbox, signed.
