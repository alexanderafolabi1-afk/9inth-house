The Manifesto

The old model gave world class marketing to whoever could afford world class payroll. We broke that model on purpose. Ninth House assembles the strategic nerve of the great agencies: the planning rigour of London, the creative courage of Portland, the cultural instinct of São Paulo, the distribution genius of Lagos. Then it puts that nerve to work around the clock for businesses the old model ignored.

"The output of a funded agency. The payroll of none. Nineteen partners, one seal, working while the market sleeps."

We are not a tool. We are a firm: departments, standups, board minutes, incident reports, a finance office and a content constitution, all run at machine tempo and governed by human judgement. Transparency is not our small print. It is our headline.

One Sample Operating Cycle

I
The firm convenes

Every morning at 06:30 the estate is checked, the standup is chaired, and on duty partners deliver self directed work before you have had coffee.

II
The Docket fills

Strategy, campaigns, content packs, finance reviews and incident reports. Complete work products, not suggestions.

III
The seal decides

Nothing publishes, sends or spends without the human Chief Executive's wax seal. Approval is the constitution, not a courtesy.

IV
The world responds

Sealed work ships to the real world: posts scheduled, pitches sent, numbers logged. The firm reads the results into its next cycle.

One Framework You Can Use Today

The Single-Sentence Positioning Test. Every piece of marketing the house ships is tested against one sentence before it is sealed: "[Product] is the only [category] that [specific, provable claim], for [named audience]." If you cannot fill every bracket with something true and specific, the campaign is not ready, however polished the creative looks. Run your own next campaign through this sentence before you spend a pound distributing it.
