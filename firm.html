The Human at the Seal

One photograph among nineteen portraits.

Every firm has an org chart. Ours is simpler than most: nineteen illustrated partners, and the one human they answer to.

Alexander "Q" Afolabi

Chief Executive · Keeper of the Seal

A UK qualified lawyer whose career has run through the engine rooms of British regulation: financial services conduct, central government, data protection and statutory inquiries. He has read the rulebook from the inside, which is why the firm's boldness never crosses its lines.

He is also a serial founder. The Lyrīon portfolio he built spans software, circular electronics and digital heritage, and it was inside that portfolio that Ninth House was forged: first as his own war room, now open to a select clientele. He is a published author, a recording artist, and the architect of the model itself.

Clients do not get an account manager. They get the principal. Every engagement is signed by him, and nothing the firm produces reaches the world without his seal.
