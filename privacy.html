Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Short version: We collect analytics data only when you say yes. We do not sell your data to anyone. We do not run advertising cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking Reject on the cookie banner, which reappears if you clear your browser storage.

Who we are

Ninth House Growth Partners is operated by Lyrion Ltd, a company incorporated in England and Wales. Our website is 9thpoint.com. You can contact us at hello@9thpoint.com.

We are the data controller for information collected through this website.

What we collect and why

We collect the following categories of information:

Analytics data (only with your consent)

  • Pages you visit and how long you spend on them
  • The country you are browsing from (not your precise location)
  • The device type, browser and operating system you use
  • Events such as clicking a payment link or opening a journal article

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect this data. GA4 uses first-party cookies to persist session information. We have configured GA4 with IP anonymisation enabled. We use this data only to understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not use it for advertising.

Messages you send us

If you contact us via the concierge chat, the WhatsApp line, or by email, we receive your message and any contact details you choose to share. We use these only to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to a mailing list without your explicit agreement.

Information you provide for a Visitor Pass

If you request a Visitor Pass inside the Tower experience on the homepage, we collect your first name and email address. This is passed to our concierge desk so we can follow up. We do not share it further.

Legal basis for processing

  • Analytics cookies: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and PECR Regulation 6)
  • Enquiry messages: legitimate interests (responding to a direct request)
  • Visitor Pass details: your explicit provision of those details for that purpose

Cookies

We use one category of cookie:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): only set after you click Accept. These cookies are named _ga, _ga_JN7VX1GFL0, and related GA4 identifiers. They expire after 2 years.

We do not set advertising, retargeting or social media tracking cookies.

You can withdraw consent at any time by reloading the page after clearing your browser's site data for 9thpoint.com, or by using your browser's built-in cookie controls.

Who we share your data with

We share analytics data with Google Ireland Ltd under a data processing agreement. Google may transfer data to its servers in the United States under standard contractual clauses approved by the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with any other third party unless required by law.

How long we keep your data

Analytics data is retained in Google Analytics for 14 months from collection, after which it is deleted automatically. Enquiry messages are retained for as long as is necessary to fulfil the enquiry, typically no longer than two years.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Object to our processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for analytics cookies)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@9thpoint.com.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if we change how we process your data. The date at the top shows when this version was last updated.
