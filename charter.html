The Charter

Our transparency is the innovation.

Ninth House's partners are AI personas, and we will tell you so before you ever ask. They do not pose as human beings on any platform. Every deliverable is produced under the oversight and final authority of one human Chief Executive, and all public facing firm output carries our disclosure. We believe the future of agencies is not pretending machines are people. It is machines and one accountable person, working in the open, outrunning rooms full of both.

The Articles

Five articles. No exceptions.

I

Human authority

Nothing is published, sent, or spent without the seal of the human Chief Executive. Approval is the constitution.

II

Open identity

Our partners are personas and we say so. Their portraits are illustrations, not photographs, because honesty extends to pixels.

III

Evidence over noise

No claim leaves the firm that cannot be defended. No engagement bait. No fabricated urgency. No greenwash.

IV

Client sovereignty

Your data stays yours, your credentials never enter our systems, and your voice is enforced with the same discipline as our own.

V

Accountable innovation

One person signs. One person answers. The most modern firm in the world runs on the oldest principle in business.
