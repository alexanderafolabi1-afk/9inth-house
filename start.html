Before You Engage

How this actually works.

No proposal deck, no discovery-call maze. Four steps, from a first message to a sealed piece of work.

IChoose an engagement
Start wherever fits: The Audit for a first look at your growth, The Boardroom Session for a working strategy hour, a Partner Pack for one specific deliverable, or a retainer for ongoing work across the firm.
IIPay through the house
Every engagement is paid directly through Ninth House's own checkout, sealed and confirmed within the hour. No procurement maze, no waiting on a sales call to raise an invoice.
IIIThe docket convenes
Named partners take your brief into the docket: the same standup discipline and the same minutes the firm keeps on its own work, every single day.
IVThe human seal
Nothing reaches you unreviewed. The Chief Executive reads and signs every piece of work before it leaves the house, in writing, every time.
