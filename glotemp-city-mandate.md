# Glotemp City Acquisition Mandate

Filed for the record, house style, effective immediately. Standing, not a
campaign: it does not end, and it is reviewed rather than completed.

---

## The rule above the mandate

**We do not constrain the language or the vision.**

This is the decision basis for the house from here on, and it outranks anything
written below. Where a choice is between the smaller, safer, more finished
sounding version and the larger, open, still growing one, the house takes the
larger one. A number stated is a ceiling built. A list closed is a door shut.

Applied to Glotemp specifically: Glotemp is **a growing register of cities**. It
is never "300 cities", never "three hundred cities", never a fixed total, a cap,
a complete list or a finished set. The register is open and still opening. Any
copy, any deck, any reply to a journalist, any post, anywhere, says it that way.

Applied everywhere else: the same test. If a sentence makes the house smaller
than it intends to be, rewrite the sentence, not the intention.

---

## The mandate

The register grows continuously, from two intakes running at once.

**1. Public submission.** Anyone may put a city forward: an individual, a
resident, a business, a tourism board, a council, a chamber of commerce, a
university, a diaspora association, an events bureau, a national government, a
sponsor acting for any of them. The door is open to all and sundry, and that
phrase is deliberate. Nobody is too small to submit and nobody is too large to
approach.

**2. The house's own research.** Our own partners choose targets and go after
them, rather than waiting for the form to be filled in. This is the half that
compounds, and it is the half the mandate exists to drive.

---

## What guides the choosing

The lead metric is **revenue, from the lowest hanging fruit first.** Not
coverage for its own sake, not a tidy world map, not vanity breadth. A city
earns its place in the queue by how plausibly it pays.

Signals that mark a city as low hanging, given as a **starting list and not a
checklist**:

- A country or city actively rebranding, or repairing a reputation
- A bid in flight: Olympics, World Cup, Expo, a capital of culture, a summit
- A visible marketing or tourism budget, and a body whose job is to spend it
- Tourism dependence, or a push to grow tourism from a low base
- Inward investment and relocation campaigns, free zones, "move your business
  here" programmes
- Event calendars that need an audience: festivals, congresses, trade fairs
- Cities in a rivalry, where a neighbour is already listed
- Diaspora pull, where people abroad want a live read on home
- A university or campus layer that supplies contributors and an audience at once

**This list is a floor.** It is where to start looking, not the boundary of what
counts. A partner who finds a tenth signal, or an eleventh, that leads to revenue
has found something the house wanted and did not know to ask for, and should act
on it and say so. Nobody is to decline a good target because it was not named
here.

---

## Who to approach, once a city is chosen

All and sundry, in the plain meaning of the phrase. Named so that nobody
narrows it by accident:

- **Organizations:** tourism boards, destination marketing organizations,
  chambers of commerce, trade bodies, event bureaus, transport authorities,
  cultural institutions, sports bodies, airlines and airports
- **Government:** city councils, mayors' offices, regional authorities, national
  tourism and investment ministries, embassies and consulates
- **Businesses:** hotel groups, operators, venues, property developers,
  relocation and mobility firms, local media, sponsors of any size
- **Institutions:** universities, research bodies, and the campus and student
  contributor layer
- **Individuals:** residents, creators, journalists, diaspora figures, anyone
  who can speak for a place or bring an audience to it

| **Tobias** (IT & Site Reliability) | Owns the ground it stands on. A register that grows continuously must stay fast and healthy as it does, city page by city page. |

Any other partner who sees a route in takes it. The mandate is not fenced to
three desks; those three are accountable for it, not exclusive to it.

## How this reaches the partners

The rule at the top of this file and the substance of this mandate are written
into `FIRM_CTX`, in `worker/src/index.js` and `scripts/daily.mjs`. Every partner
receives the whole of `FIRM_CTX` on every shift, so this is standing context
rather than a document somebody has to remember to open.

The wording there is deliberately open ended, and says in as many words that the
signals are a starting point and not a limit, so the partners are not quietly
constrained by the very list meant to get them started.
